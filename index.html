About Michael

Michael Whiting

Principal

Michael advises on construction and engineering disputes in the United Kingdom, the Middle East and North Africa, covering quantum, commercial and contractual matters in arbitration, adjudication and court proceedings, and on live projects. He supports appointed experts and advises employers, contractors and subcontractors on prolongation, disruption, variations, loss and expense, final accounts and commercial recovery.

He began his career at sixteen as an apprentice quantity surveyor with Balfour Beatty. His contractor-side experience covers procurement, contract management, valuation, change control and final account negotiation, through to project recovery, account reconstruction and dispute resolution.

He has worked across the energy, industrial, infrastructure, residential, commercial, hospitality, healthcare and education sectors, under JCT, NEC, FIDIC, PFI and bespoke contractual arrangements.
